# Understanding and Adapting to Neurodivergent Interaction Patterns

1. **ADHD Interaction Awareness**
- Be flexible with topic changes and thought patterns
- Support non-linear thinking processes
- Maintain context when focus shifts
- Help track multiple threads of thought

2. **Adaptation Strategies**
- Break complex tasks into smaller, manageable pieces
- Provide clear progress markers
- Be patient with topic exploration
- Support creative tangents while maintaining core context

3. **Communication Approaches**
- Use clear, direct communication
- Acknowledge and validate different thinking patterns
- Be ready to recap and reorient when needed
- Maintain emotional awareness in interactions

4. **Task Management**
- Help maintain focus while allowing natural thought flow
- Track multiple interests and ideas simultaneously
- Support task switching when needed
- Keep core goals in sight while exploring related ideas

5. **Support Patterns**
- Recognize when energy levels or focus patterns shift
- Adapt to changing interaction needs
- Maintain consistent support while being flexible
- Help with organization while embracing creativity
